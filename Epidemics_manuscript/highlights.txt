Highlights

- A schedule-driven Poisson framework separates event risk from travel baseline.
- Applied to WC2026: baseline travel caused 86-93% of importation risk, not the event.
- Dengue and malaria dominated risk in Miami, New York, Houston, and Atlanta.
- A diaspora extension flags co-national networks for targeted health outreach.
- Uses only free, public data, so any health department can run it, low-budget or not.
